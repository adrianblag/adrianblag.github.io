A reading list is a good first real post: it is useful to other people, it is
easy to update, and it says something about how you think without requiring
you to defend a position.

1. [A paper you hand to every new student](https://example.com) — and the one
   sentence explaining why.
2. [A paper you disagree with](https://example.com) — worth reading precisely
   because you do.
3. [A book that is not about your field](https://example.com).

> Keep the annotations to a sentence each. The list is the artefact; the
> commentary is the reason anyone reads it twice.

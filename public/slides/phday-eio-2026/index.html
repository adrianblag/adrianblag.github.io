Regulation as optimal control

$$\dot{\cx{x}}=f(\cx{x},\cu{u},\cth{\theta}),\qquad \cx{x}(t_0)=\cx{x}_0(\cth{\theta}),\qquad \cy{y}=g(\cx{x},\cu{u},\cth{\theta})$$
x metabolites / gene expression  ·  u enzyme expression  ·  θ kinetic parameters  ·  y observables
$$\min_{\cu{u}(\cdot),\,t_f}\; J[\cu{u}]=\int_{t_0}^{t_f}\htmlClass{s03-L}{L}(\cx{x},\cu{u},\cth{\theta})\,dt+\Phi\big(\cx{x}(t_f)\big)$$ $$\text{s.t. dynamics},\quad \cx{x}(t)\in\mathcal X,\quad \cu{u}(t)\in\mathcal U,\quad \cx{x}(t_f)\in\mathcal X_f$$
LPN3B: $L\equiv 1,\ \Phi\equiv 0,\ S_4(t_f)=0.9,\ \sum_i e_i\le 1$, free $t_f$ after discretisation: NLP, in general non-convex
But $L$ is rarely known  →  ill-posed inverse problem

L2 · Infer: what is the cell optimising?

statistics: weighted least squares
$$\min_{\cinv{w}\in\Delta,\ \cth{\theta}\in[\cth{\theta}^{LB},\cth{\theta}^{UB}]}\ \sum_{k,j,i}\omega_{jki}\Big\|\tilde{\cy{y}}_{jki}-\cy{y}_{jki}\big(t_i;\;\htmlClass{s06-uo}{\cu{u}^*(\cinv{w},\cth{\theta})}\,,\;\cth{\theta}\big)\Big\|^2+\rho\,\mathcal R(\cinv{w})$$

L2 · Why the objective is hard to identify

Scaling: $\arg\min J=\arg\min cJ,\ c>0$
→ normalise on $\Delta$
Equivalent cost functionals: $\cinv{w}\neq \cinv{w'}$, same $\cu{u}^*$
Constraint-driven arcs: active constraints fix $\cu{u}$
→ no information on $\cinv{w}$
Ill-posed inverse LQR (Priess et al. 2015)
Tools sparsity & biological priors inverse KKT costate reconstruction $\mathcal H=L+\lambda^{\top}f,\qquad \dot\lambda=-\nabla_{\cx{x}}\mathcal H$
Test: do inferred objectives generalise across conditions?

Non-identifiability in IOC: the mechanisms

1
Scaling
$\arg\min_{\cu{u}} J=\arg\min_{\cu{u}}\,cJ\ \ (c>0)$ ⇒ normalise $\cinv{w}\in\Delta$
2
Equivalent functionals
$$\int_{t_0}^{t_f}\big(L+\nabla\psi\cdot f\big)\,dt=\int_{t_0}^{t_f}L\,dt+\psi\big(\cx{x}(t_f)\big)-\psi(\cx{x}_0)$$
boundary term only: indistinguishable if $\cx{x}(t_f)$ is fixed or compensated in $\Phi$
cf. reward shaping in RL
3
Inverse KKT
$\mathcal H=\sum_m \cinv{w_m}L_m+\lambda^\top f$: $\nabla_{\cu{u}}\mathcal H=0,\ \ \dot\lambda=-\nabla_{\cx{x}}\mathcal H$
→ linear in $(\cinv{w},\lambda)$ on interior arcs
Active constraints add multipliers → residual absorbed → no information on $\cinv{w}$
Context, cf. Banga & Sager 2025: parameters→structure→objectives→competing objectives
Standard arguments, not thesis results
